About Me

I’m Om Mandhane, a Robotics Engineering Student passionate about Embodied AI and Robot Learning. My recent work focuses on the fine-tuning and deployment of end-to-end VLA policies for robotic manipulation, supported by custom low-latency teleoperation systems I engineered for efficient data collection.

I am particularly interested in leveraging World Models, foundation models, and diffusion policies to create robots that can simulate and understand the physical world to perform complex, generalizable tasks.
