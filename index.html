Research interests

focus areas

My research interest sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, world models, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and humanoid manipulation. I'm interested in generalist manipulation policies that anticipate the consequences of their actions rather than only reacting — using a predictive world model to imagine how a scene and its objects will change before an action is taken, and correcting behavior against new observations as they come in. This matters most for humanoid robots, where manipulation involves complex contacts and whole-body motion: I want to study whether predictive world models can make VLA/RL policies more sample-efficient, more robust, and better at generalizing to new tasks, particularly for bimanual and whole-body manipulation.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • World models
  • Vision-Language-Action models
  • Humanoid manipulation
  • Whole-body & bimanual control
  • Generalist, command-driven policies

Phone2Act — hardware-agnostic teleoperation for VLA data collection

A low-cost 6-DoF teleoperation framework that turns a commodity smartphone into a spatial controller via ARCore, built to address the demonstration-data bottleneck in vision-language-action robotics.

  • Modular three-layer ROS 2 architecture (interface, planner, hardware bridge) decoupled through standardized topics, with workspace clamping and a zero-jump safety filter for real-robot deployment.
  • Universal Recorder synchronizing multi-camera streams with 20Hz robot state, exporting directly to LeRobot dataset format (MP4/Parquet) — a turnkey pipeline from teleoperation to training.
  • Validated on single-arm (Dobot CR5) and bimanual (LeRobot SO-101) platforms at 395ms end-to-end latency; collected 130 episodes to fine-tune NVIDIA's GR00T-N1.5-3B diffusion policy with 10-step temporal action chunking, deployed via a cloud-based inference bridge.

Autonomous navigation with model predictive control

A real-time ROS 2 local planner solving a receding-horizon optimization problem with CasADi/IPOPT under unicycle kinematic constraints, so every planned trajectory is physically executable.

  • Dynamic obstacle avoidance via an inverse-barrier cost function over downsampled 2D LiDAR scans mapped into repulsive potential fields.
  • Sparse waypoints pre-processed into C²-continuous reference paths with SciPy cubic B-splines, plus a monotonic progress tracker to prevent path-jumping on sharp hairpin maneuvers.
  • A cruise-control cost function to penalize deceleration and keep forward progress, with solver warm-starting inertia to eliminate saddle-point oscillations.
